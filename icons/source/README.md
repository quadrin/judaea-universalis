# Judaea Universalis lion artwork

`lion-title.png` is the approved original title drawing, preserved unchanged.
`lion-head.png` is its icon derivative. Both are PNG masters with transparency,
created with the built-in image-generation tool.

Title direction: a rampant, left-facing Lion of Judah in warm gold and charcoal
woodcut engraving, above JUDAEA / UNIVERSALIS in Roman serif capitals. Keep the
lion and wordmark together, with no enclosing shield, tile, coin or badge.

Icon direction: isolate the approved lion's left-facing head and curling mane;
preserve the face, gold ink, incised outlines and engraving character. Remove
the body and lettering, simplify the finest lines, and retain transparency.

The title screen uses the complete approved drawing. Browser and installed-game
icons use the head. `bash tools/build_icons.sh` exports the web artwork using
ImageMagick; maskable icons keep their content within the central safe area.
