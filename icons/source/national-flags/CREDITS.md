# National flag artwork

`manifest.json` identifies each original file, its contributors, rights declaration,
and dated identity. The corresponding SVG files preserve the retrieved drawings.
`tools/build_national_flags.py` removes editorial metadata, rejects active or
external content, and fits the entire flag into the court chip without stretching.
The source file hashes, credits, and flag-icons license are embedded in the game
module, including the portable offline HTML.

The seven flag-icons files are by Panayiotis Lipiridis and contributors, under the
MIT license reproduced in `LICENSE-flag-icons.txt`.

The Spanish flag vector by SanchoPanzaXXI is used under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Its adaptation
(metadata removal, passive SVG serialization and display fitting) remains under
that license. This does not change the license of the rest of the game.

The Hezbollah flag is a copyrighted organizational symbol, reproduced solely to
identify that faction in the game's historical interface. Its source page and
vector contributors are listed in the manifest. No free license or public-domain
status is claimed for that asset. It is not an endorsement.

The remaining retrieved flag files carry the public-domain declarations recorded
on their linked source pages. Those pages describe the graphics; historical
identity notes retain the distinction between national flags, merchant ensigns,
and retrospective faction identities.

For the occupation-era German court, the C-pennant represents the documented
merchant ensign prescribed by Allied Control Council Law No. 39 (12 November
1946), rather than a fictional national flag. Yemen's five stars and sword are
also shown in the February 1951 National Geographic flag chart. French-mandate
flags follow the period designs described in the 1939 Flaggenbuch and French
naval archival material. Relevant specialist transcriptions:

- https://www.crwflags.com/FOTW/flags/ye_kingd.html
- https://www.crwflags.com/FOTW/FLAGS/de1945.html
- https://www.crwflags.net/FOTW/flags/sy-alw20.html
- https://www.crwflags.com/fotw/FLAGS/sy-dru22.html
